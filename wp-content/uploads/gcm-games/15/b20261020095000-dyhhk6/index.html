How to Play

  • Goal: break every brick on screen to clear the level.
  • Move: ← → or A / D — or drag on the board, or hold the ◀ ▶ buttons in the bar below it. Space or a tap on the board launches the ball.
  • Lives: you start with 3 lives. Each missed ball costs one.
  • Power-ups: catch the falling capsules with your paddle — Multi-ball, Bigger Paddle, Slow Ball, Fireball and Bomb. Chain brick hits without touching the paddle in between for combo coins.
  • Bricks: darker ones take several hits; dashed ones slide. Brick coins are lost if you give up at Game Over.
  • There are 35 levels. Clearing one unlocks the next; no continue earns 3 stars.
  • Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over — you can watch a short ad to continue where you were with 3 fresh lives, as many times as you like.
  • Shop: spend coins on paddle and ball skins, brick themes and backgrounds.
