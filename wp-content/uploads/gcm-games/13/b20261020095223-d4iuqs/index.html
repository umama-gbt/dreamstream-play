How to Play

  • The board is divided into 9x9 cells, grouped into 3x3 smaller boxes.
  • Each row, each column, and each 3x3 box must contain every number from 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Tap an empty cell, then choose a number from the pad below.
  • Entering a wrong number will highlight the cell in red and count as a mistake.
  • After 5 mistakes it's Game Over: retry the level, or watch a short ad to continue the same board with your mistakes reset.
  • Use the cross (X) button to clear the number in the selected cell.
  • Fill the entire board correctly to complete the level and unlock the next one.
  • Difficulty increases with each level (Easy → Medium → Hard → Very Hard → Expert) as the number of clues decreases.
  • Use the speaker icon to turn sound on or off.
  • Earn coins for every level you finish and spend them in the Shop on themes, number styles and backgrounds.
