How to Play

  • Goal: reveal every safe tile without hitting a mine.
  • Controls: left click a tile to reveal it. Right click to place a ⚑ flag, again for a ? mark, once more to clear it. Double click a number to open its neighbors once you've flagged that many mines around it. On a phone or tablet: tap to reveal, press and hold to flag, double-tap a number.
  • Numbers: each one tells you how many mines touch that tile. An empty tile opens up all the connected empty tiles.
  • Your first click is always safe — the mines are placed after it.
  • There are 30 levels, from a 15×10 Beginner board up to a 31×24 Expert board. Win a level to unlock the next one.
  • Rewards: 20 coins for clearing a board, +20 for no wrong flags, +15 for a fast clear.
  • 3 mistakes allowed: each mine you hit counts as a mistake and is marked on the board. Hit a 3rd mine and it is Game Over — watch a short ad to continue right where you left off with all 3 mistakes restored, or retry the level.
  • Customize: spend coins on board themes, tile designs and flag designs.
