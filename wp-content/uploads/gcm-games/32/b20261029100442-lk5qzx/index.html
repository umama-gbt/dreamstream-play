How to Play

  • Goal: roll every marble into the dashed ring in the middle of the maze.
  • Tilt: drag the joystick in the bar — or hold the arrow keys or WASD — and the whole maze tilts that way. Let go and it levels out.
  • Black holes: one marble in a hole is Game Over. From level 15 some of them move.
  • Timer: each maze has its own time limit, from 0:35 on level 1 up to 3:20. It starts with your first tilt.
  • Coins: +20 for each clear, +15 with a quarter of the time left and +10 if no two marbles bump.
  • There are 35 mazes in 5 worlds — Grass, Desert, Ice, Space and Lava. Clearing one unlocks the next.
  • Game Over? Watch a short ad to carry on exactly where you were with the full time back, as many times as you like.
  • Shop: spend coins on ball skins, maze themes and trail effects.
