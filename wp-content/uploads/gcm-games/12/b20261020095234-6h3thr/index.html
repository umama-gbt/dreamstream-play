How to Play

  • Goal: stack the falling blocks and clear horizontal lines before the board fills up.
  • Move: ← → slide a piece, ↓ drops it faster, ↑ rotates it — or use the on-screen joystick on a phone or tablet. Esc (or Pause) pauses anytime.
  • Levels: there are 35 levels. Clear 10 lines to finish one and the pieces drop faster on the next. The ← ↻ → buttons under the board go back, restart (after a short ad) or move on.
  • Rewards: finishing a level earns 🌟 stars and 🪙 coins, with a bonus for clearing several rows at once and for combos. Unlock Achievements along the way.
  • Shop: spend coins on block skins and unlock achievements along the way.
  • Game over: it happens when a new piece has nowhere to spawn — you can watch a short ad to continue where you were, as many times as you like.
  • Progress resets 24 hours after your session begins. After a reset, tap a locked level on Select Level and watch 5 ads to unlock it — only that level opens.
  • Reaching a level unlocks it as a starting level for next time.
