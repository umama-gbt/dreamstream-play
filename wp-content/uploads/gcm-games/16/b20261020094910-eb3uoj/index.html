How to Play

  • Goal: climb until your score reaches the level's target to clear it.
  • Move: arrow keys or A / D — or the on-screen arrows if you are on a phone or tablet. Your doodler jumps by itself every time it lands.
  • Platforms: green are solid, brown crumble the moment you jump off, blue slide side to side.
  • Points: every new highest platform you land on is worth +5.
  • Coins: grab the coins floating above platforms — they are yours when you clear the level.
  • There are 35 levels. Clearing one unlocks the next, which climbs higher and gets trickier.
  • You can make 3 mistakes (shown as lives). Falling off the bottom costs one and puts you back on the platform you fell from, with your score kept. After the 3rd, watch an ad all the way through to get all 3 back — as many times as you like.
  • Shop: spend coins on doodler skins; milestone skins unlock when you clear levels 10, 20, 30 and 35.
