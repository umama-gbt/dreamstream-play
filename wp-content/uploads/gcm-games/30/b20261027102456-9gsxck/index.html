How to Play

  • Goal: guess the hidden word one letter at a time, before the figure is complete.
  • Guess: tap a letter on the on-screen keyboard, or type it on your keyboard.
  • Right letter: it fills every matching blank in the word.
  • Wrong letter: it adds one part to the figure. Every level allows 6 wrong guesses — the 6th completes the figure. The words get harder as the levels go up.
  • Word packs: pick a pack first (Animals, Food, Objects, Places or Science), then a level. All 5 packs share the same 40 levels and your progress; only the words change.
  • Coins: +25 the first time you clear a level, and +20 more if you clear it with no wrong guesses.
  • Out of guesses? Watch a short ad to get all 6 guesses back on the same word — as many times as you like.
  • Shop: spend coins on figure colours, board backgrounds and new word packs — some packs unlock with a short ad instead.
