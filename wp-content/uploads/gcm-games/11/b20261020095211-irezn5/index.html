How to Play

  • Goal: eat every pellet in the maze to clear the level.
  • Move: arrow keys or WASD — or the on-screen joystick if you are on a phone or tablet. Push it the way you want Pac-Man to turn.
  • Ghosts: you start with 3 lives. Touching a ghost costs one.
  • Power Pellet: eat a big dot and every ghost turns scared for a few seconds — 10 seconds on the first levels, down to 4 seconds on the last ones — touch them now and you eat them instead.
  • Points: pellet +10, power pellet +50. Ghosts eaten during one power pellet are worth 200, then 400, 800, 1600.
  • There are 30 levels. Clearing one unlocks the next and earns you coins.
  • Lose all 3 lives and it is Game Over — you can watch a short ad to continue where you were with 3 fresh lives, as many times as you like.
  • Shop: spend coins on Pac-Man skins, maze themes, ghost themes and effects.
